# M6: enemy roster

All numbers are trial settings, calibrated against the doc 25 and 27 targets with the honest exceptions listed below. No art exists for any of these enemies.

## What was built

Every id in `combat/rooms/ENCOUNTER_CONTRACT.json` is now an entry of `SIM.ENCOUNTERS` with the same archetypes in the same order: `harrier`, `harrierSentry`, `harrierMireling`, `spitter`, `spitterGrunt`, `sentrySpitter`, `brute`, `duelist`, `warden`, `castellan`, plus the older four. One extra sim-only id, `gruntAlone`, covers the grunt of a `spitterGrunt` room when it alone sees the adventurer.

| Enemy | HP | Speed | Moves (windup / recovery, damage) | Behaviour |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Tower Harrier | 72 | 2.6 | aimed shot (31 / 22, 18), quick shot (14 / 16, 9) | Keeps range, circles, moves to a clear lane when it loses sight. In company it re-seats after every shot (`groupSlow` 2.4). |
| Silt Spitter | 78 | 2.3 | silt bolt (24 / 18, 19), spit (22 / 22, no direct damage) | Backs away when approached. Spit leaves a marked circle (radius 92) that warns 24 ticks, lasts 100, and ticks 5 damage and 5 stamina every 9 ticks. At most one live patch per spitter. |
| Mudjaw Brute | 185 | 1.75 | wide swing (22 / 32, 28), slam (30 / 44, 40), charge (22 / 34, 30) | Walks in. The charge is committed and cannot steer; hitting a solid stuns it. |
| Crypt Duelist | 105 | 3.2 | two cuts, thrust, counter (windup 8 to 15, damage 14 to 18) | Fast combo, sidesteps, counters a swing thrown into it. |
| Bell Warden (miniboss) | 340 | 1.8 | sweep (26 / 30, 26), gatebreaker (38 / 44, 38), toll (32 / 50, 7, radial pulse) | Radial toll pulses; below half health the second ring follows after a clear gap. |
| Siltbound Castellan (boss) | 560 | 1.55 | arc (30 / 36, 27), slam (40 / 52, 40), silt wave (36 / 40, 17, wide projectile), flood (18 / 40) | Flood marks one lane (two in phase 2, one after the other, never all three) on a fixed cadence. Phase 2 below 45% health. |

New engine pieces, all optional and silent unless an enemy uses them: ground hazards (`S.hazards`, circle or rect, warned, timed, with damage and stamina ticks; a dodge's i-frames pass through them), a charge crash stun, radial pulses, boss phases, and `CAUSES.hazard` / `dmgBy.hazard` so hazard damage is blamed like any other hit.

One change to the adventurer: against a ranged enemy that is not fodder, and when no window or threat is readable, the offense score gains `0.5 * (1 - patience)`. Before this, a guarded adventurer with low greed never attacked a harrier or spitter at all (survivor focus, guarded, L140: 11% against a harrier, 1% against a spitter, 10 to 20 second deaths with no swing thrown). It touches only fights that contain a non-fodder ranged enemy, so every legacy encounter is unchanged: `regress.js` prints `identical 2340, different 0` and `legacy-check.js` prints `identical 78, different 0`.

## World layer

- `ENCOUNTER_FOR` in `src/world/roomRunner.ts` is now built from the contract, so a new encounter id needs no code change. A pair listed the other way round in a room is matched and reordered.
- `SOLO` (brute, duelist, warden, castellan) fight alone: they never take an ally into a fight and no regular enemy joins them.
- `RoomResult.bossesDefeated` and `WorldResult.bossesDefeated` list the warden and castellan killed on that visit. `buildChronicle` passes them on, and `settle` already writes them to `world.defeatedBosses` whatever the outcome.
- A boss left alive (a retreat that breaks contact) goes back to its post unengaged and is started again at full health by the next fight. Nothing carries damage between fights.
- `KILL_LOOT` has trial entries for every roster enemy.
- `tests/golden/world-runs.json` was re-recorded. The only change is the new `"bossesDefeated": []` field on each of the 24 runs; no digest, outcome or number moved.

## Exceptions, stated plainly

- Pressing doctrine sits under the band on the high-level targets for harrier, brute, warden and castellan. Pressing walks into every swing; I did not weaken the other doctrines to level it.
- The castellan is under its L140 target for balanced focus (25 / 31 / 15%). Raising it would mean cutting the castellan's damage or HP, which makes the L1 to L80 fights even more of a formality than a wall. I left it hard. If you want 30 to 55% for balanced at L140, drop castellan HP from 560 toward 500 first.
- Guarded beats measured on the elites and miniboss. This is a property of the existing doctrine (it waits for windows), not of the roster.
- Pair encounters have no doc target. Results at the level a room would be met: `harrierSentry` 4% at L80 and 54% at L140 (measured); `harrierMireling` 20% at L80, 63% at L140; `spitterGrunt` 38% at L80, 74% at L140; `sentrySpitter` 0% at L80, 14% at L140. `sentrySpitter` is the hardest regular encounter and K1 sits behind the brute room; if the slice is meant to be played at L80, it is too hard there. The old `sentryMireling` is 32% at L80 for comparison.
- Survivor focus at L140 under the guarded doctrine against a spitter is 74%, lower than its neighbours (84 at L80, 94 measured at L140). Not investigated further.
