# M5: progression and the sanctuary

Branch `claude/m5-progression`. Trial settings throughout. Nothing here is tuned or approved, and no human, device or combat QA has been done.

## What exists

Temporary levels. An expedition starts at a design level (1 to 9) and gains experience from kills, from health lost in won fights, from completed objectives and from first-time rooms. The level goes through an editable table to a simulator level. Levels, experience and the sanctuary upgrade end with the expedition. The save has no new fields, and a test checks that none of them reach it. Mastery stays the only banked progress.

Rations. Two starter rations per expedition (not loot, never banked, never cached) plus any found. The plan has a drink threshold and a healing reserve. A ration heals 35% of maximum health.

The sanctuary. One, in S2 (Broken Pump), placed by `combat/rooms/overrides/S2.json`. The first visit takes the plan's upgrade choice. The first time the adventurer is hurt there (below 90% health) it rests once, for 60% health and full stamina. It never banks, never protects a cache and never extracts. The screen says "No banking here".

Tools: `tools/level-calibrate.ts` (takes `--targets`, `--anchors`, `--write`) and `tools/progression-report.ts` (writes `M5_REPORT.md`).

## Decisions

Progression is opt-in. `ExpeditionConfig.progression` turns it on. Without it, fights, digests and both goldens behave as before. The page turns it on by default; `?progress=0` turns it off, and the old browser specs use that. `?startlevel=N` sets the start level.

The simulator is untouched. An upgrade is a handful of stat points added after `allocate` (capped at 20 per stat), so there is no new combat rule. Regress and legacy-check both print `different 0`.

Levels apply at arrival in a room. `refreshStats` runs at `beginLeg`, never mid-fight, and keeps the share of health. Maximum health is recomputed from the new stats.

Experience values (kills 8 for a Mireling, 20 for a Sentry, 12 per objective, 4 per new room, up to 25% extra on a won leg by health lost) and thresholds (10, 24, 44, 70, 105, 150, 205, 270) are guesses chosen so a level 1 hero reaches level 2 on the Outer Road. I halved the first thresholds after the first report because progress per expedition was close to zero.

Drinking rule: travelling only, below the plan threshold, no enemy within 90 units, 20 ticks of standing still, one at a time, never mid-attack. Each drink is logged with its cause. With the threshold at 0 nothing is drunk.

Retreat versus drinking: the retreat check runs at each arrival, the drink during travel. A plan with its drink threshold below its retreat threshold goes home before it drinks. That is how the rule reads and I kept it, but it is easy to set wrong in the UI. A hint or a clamp is not built.

The healing reserve sends the adventurer home when rations are at or below the reserve, but only after at least one has been drunk, so a plan never turns back at the gate for carrying few.

The upgrade choice is a plan field with a default (`steadfast`), as the brief asked for no pause.

## Findings

Numbers are from `M5_REPORT.md` (300 seeds a row).

1. The existing encounters are a poor level ladder. A lone Mireling wins 93% at level 1. A lone Sentry sits near 68% from level 1 to 40. Only the two pairs climb with level, so the table is calibrated on those: L1=15, L2=48, L3=62, L4=94. Rows 5 to 9 are extrapolated at +12 simulator levels per design level and are marked uncalibrated in the table and in `M5_CALIBRATION.md`. Doc 27's boss band (true level 40 to 65) is lower than what the Sentry and Mireling pair needs for 55% (about 94), so the doc and the roster disagree. M6's roster has to settle this, then re-run `npx tsx tools/level-calibrate.ts --targets <file> --write`.

2. Level 1 cannot do the Old Road. A cautious level 1 plan to O3 and KT completes all objectives 0% of the time. It retreats after the first fight at 60% health, extracts 99% of runs, and carries about 5.5 gold. Level 2 reaches the sanctuary 5% of the time, level 4 17% (cautious) and 5% (pressing). So the sanctuary is real but rarely reached on the placeholder map. The route to S2 is long and the retreat setting turns most runs around.

3. Safe farming across expeditions exists and is not new. The shallow plan (survey the Outer Road) extracts 99.7% of the time, completes its objective 99.7%, and levels to 2 with 6 gold banked. Each expedition respawns the enemies, so repeating it pays 6 gold a time at almost no risk. Inside one expedition kills pay once. M5 changes nothing here, but experience makes no difference to it either, since levels reset. A cap or a diminishing return on repeated shallow runs is a decision for the loot owner.

4. Rations help little and the evidence is thin. Pressing Old Road: died 15.7% with starter rations against 18.0% without, a 2.3 point gap at 300 seeds, which is about the size of the noise. Cautious: 1.0% against 1.0%. Mean gold banked moves by 0.2 to 0.4. They are not dominant. If they are meant to matter, the heal or the number of starter rations has to go up, or the threshold default has to be higher than 50.

5. Upgrades at level 62 (isolated fights, 300 seeds). Riposte is the strongest: Sentry plus Mireling guarded 50.7% to 67.0%, pressing 8.0% to 16.7%. Steadfast adds 3 to 9 points. Quickstep is about zero (51.7% and 8.0% against 50.7% and 8.0%, and 51.0% against 52.7% on the Mireling pair). Riposte dominates. Quickstep is a dead choice. I did not rebalance, because the upgrades are placeholder stat bundles and the real versions need a simulator rule.

6. Pacing. A level 1 start gains about 0.1 levels on the Old Road (mean level 1.06 to 1.18) because it retreats early. The shallow plan reaches level 2 every time. A level 2 start gains 0.25 levels on the long route. Progress per expedition is steady only at the Outer Road. Deeper rooms need the M6 roster for XP values, and the thresholds above will move.

## Tests

`tests/progression.test.ts`: 18 tests. They cover the table, the experience rules, kit rations first, the ledger, no progression leaking into the save (a settled expedition has no `xp`, `sanctuary`, `upgrade` keys), a fall banking nothing, the drinking rule (off at 0, spacing at least 20 ticks, logged), starter rations never banked or cached, the healing reserve, one sanctuary in S2, one upgrade and one rest per expedition from the plan default, the settled bank equal to what was carried, and determinism.

Not covered by a direct test: "a level applies at arrival and not mid-fight" is only checked loosely (the feed entry is not earlier than the gain) and "health share kept across a level-up" has no direct test. The drinking-threshold bound (never above the threshold) is checked by construction, not asserted per drink.

Browser: three new specs at 1280x720, 390x844 and 844x390 (controls, level line, sanctuary line, Chronicle progress line, no sideways scroll, no progression keys in the exported save), one spec for `?progress=0`, and the page-versus-Node parity spec now includes seven progression cases.

Golden: `tests/golden/expedition-runs.json` re-recorded for the 12 runs. I compared old and new: the only differences are the Chronicle version (3 to 4), the three new plan fields and `progress: null`, 60 field changes in all. No digest or result changed. `tests/golden/world-runs.json` is unchanged.

## Checks run

lint, `tsc --noEmit`, `npm test` (157 pass), `npm run build`, regress (`identical 2340, different 0`), legacy-check (`identical 78, different 0`), `sync-portable-math.js --check` (in sync), browser specs on Chrome 156 (37 pass).

`npm run world:check` fails, and not because of this work: it reports `ENCOUNTER_CONTRACT.json (not generated)`. The parent's commit 14d3573 added that file to `combat/rooms/`, and the check treats every non-generated JSON in that folder as stray. After `world:build` the room files are current. Move the contract file out of that folder or teach the check to skip it.

## Not built

- A calibrated table above design level 4.
- A clamp or hint for a drink threshold below the retreat threshold.
- A direct test for level timing and health share at a level-up.
- Any rebalance of rations or upgrades.
- Any change to the loot loop for repeated shallow expeditions.
