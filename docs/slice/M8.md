## What a boss is

`src/world/bosses.ts` holds the two fights: the Bell Warden in MB (miniboss, gate T2~MB) and the Siltbound Castellan in B1 (boss, gate K2~B1). Each has one gear item that only the first clear pays: `warden-bell-clapper`, `castellan-silt-crest`.

Commitment. A boss is fought only through a `challenge` or `rematch` objective. The Expedition bars every living boss arena from routes, exploring and Thorough sweeps. A `reach` or `survey` of a room that holds a living boss is skipped as blocked. The preflight blocks the same cases. A challenge walks toward the boss from the entry door until it sees the adventurer. The fight is the simulator's. The boss fights alone (`SOLO`).

Gate and key. A challenge never opens a gate. It needs an earlier `open` objective in the same plan, or a gate already open in the save. The preflight says which: gate state, whether a banked key is needed, and the boss status. With no key and no open gate, the challenge is skipped as blocked and the plan goes on to its next objective.

Retreat is physical. In MB the shortcut door to K2 stays shut until the Warden falls, so the only way out is the door in. Retreating from a fight uses the existing disengage. Leaving a boss alive resets it: it goes back to its post at full health on the next visit, nothing is cleared, no key comes back, and the Chronicle records a `boss_reset`. Dying never closes a gate that was opened.

First clear. It is permanent the tick the boss dies. In-run, the Warden's death activates link MB~K2 straight away, so the way home can use it. `settle` writes `defeatedBosses` and the opened link in every outcome (extracted, died), and does it once: settling the same expedition twice is refused by the existing `startedAt` check. The Castellan opens no link. Its campaign-route unlock is `defeatedBosses` containing `castellan`, and the Chronicle's `bosses.world` lists `campaign-route-unlocked`. I did not add a save field for it, so the save stays version 2. A defeated boss stays absent: its arena is empty on every later pass unless a rematch puts a temporary one there.

Death and a kill on the same tick. The simulator checks the kill first and reports `adventurer` as the winner. `RoomRunner` now treats `winner === 'adventurer'` with health at 0 as a defeat in which the kill stands. The enemy stays dead, the boss counts as defeated and pays, and the adventurer is dead. Tested with the real simulator wrapped so that one Warden fight ends this way.

Rewards. A kill banks nothing. Gold, mastery and the one gear item go into what is carried, at risk until camp. Clear plus death keeps the clear and puts the reward in the one recovery cache. Chronicle `events` records `gate_opened`, `boss_engaged`, `boss_defeated`, `boss_reset`, and `loot_banked` or `loot_cached` as separate lines.

## Rematch

Opt-in only, and only after the first clear: a rematch before the first clear is skipped as blocked, and a first-time challenge on a cleared boss is skipped, not counted as done (doc 15, B13). The arena gets a temporary boss for that expedition. No key is charged. The first-clear flag is never touched. Reward choice: a quarter of the kill's gold and mastery (rounded down), no gear (`REMATCH_GOLD_SHARE`). Limited-gold was my call. A full-gold rematch is a farm, and no gear keeps the first clear the only source of the item.

The one challenge that does not revert the clear is the `limited-healing` rematch variant: no starter rations and no drinking all expedition. Win or lose, the clear stands (`settle` can only add to `defeatedBosses` and `openedLinks`). Other challenges (a timed fight, a no-armour fight) are not built.

## Chronicle and UI

Chronicle is version 5. New fields: `bosses` (null unless a boss was engaged: engaged, first clears, rematches with variant and result, and the world change recorded) and `events`. `bossesDefeated` lists first clears only. Settlement lines announce each first clear once.

The planner screen gained the Challenge and Rematch actions, a rematch variant select, and a line list of Chronicle events under the settlement. Nothing else moved in `expeditionScreen.ts` or `expedition.css`.

## Findings

The bosses are close to unreachable as shipped. Under the default 40% retreat setting a level 9 adventurer wins against the Warden in 1% of expeditions and against the Castellan in 0%. The retreat rule fires on the road, so most expeditions never meet the boss. Turning the retreat off does not rescue it: the Warden falls 31% of the time at level 9 and the Castellan 3%. At level 9 in a clean arena the Warden is a 71% fight and the Castellan 35%, so the road to the boss costs more than the boss. I did not break the road down room by room, so I cannot say which rooms do the damage; the M6 balance notes already show the K1 sentry-and-spitter at 0% for a level 80 adventurer. This is a calibration problem for the level table and the encounter set, not for the boss code. I did not touch the level table, the calibrator or the simulator.

Level 1 to 3 adventurers cannot do any of this. Level 1 wins 2% of arena fights against the Warden.
