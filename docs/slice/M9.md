# M9: experience and polish

Everything here is built and checked in software: unit tests, Playwright on Chrome 156, headless timings. Nothing was tried on a real phone, with a screen reader, or by ear. Those checks are listed at the end as pending.

The simulator, `worldRunner` and `settle` were not touched. The screen still only reads what the expedition layer reports and never picks a tactic.

## What shipped

**Chronicle screen** (`src/ui/chronicleView.ts`). The result card is now a readable page: the walk drawn on a map with numbered stops and as a list, every decision with its cause, what was gained by source (kills, finds, cache), fights, Gate Keys by provenance (issued, spent, unspent, fresh), what the expedition leaves for good (mapped rooms, gates opened, one-time finds, bosses), and what happened to the loot. After a fall it names the cause from the simulator's own blame (`CAUSES`, `dmgBy`, the last hit). The cause is read from the fight state in the UI because the Chronicle does not carry it. The old ids (`#outcome`, `#summary`, `#settle-lines`, `#progress-line`, `#download`, `#again`) and the "Opened for good" lines are kept.

**History** (`src/ui/history.ts`). The last 20 Chronicles, newest first, in their own browser storage key (`idle-souls.history`), capped at 1.5 MB of text. A damaged or missing key reads as empty. It is not part of the save, so clearing it cannot touch a bank, a cache or the map. "Past expeditions" lists them; each opens the same Chronicle page. Export of a single Chronicle is unchanged.

**Camp map** (`src/ui/mapScreen.ts`, `src/ui/mapDraw.ts`). One drawing function now serves the run view, the camp map and the Chronicle route, so a mark means the same everywhere. It shows entered rooms, sealed and open gates, the recovery cache, danger marks, avoided rooms and the edge of the map. Tapping a room selects it; "Reach it", "Survey it", "Explore from here" (edge rooms) and "Avoid it" are visible buttons. Long press toggles Avoid as a shortcut only. Arrow keys move between rooms, Escape clears, and a collapsed list of every room as buttons covers keyboard and screen readers. The route preview has three states in the preflight's own words: known route (solid line), partly unmapped (dashed, stops at the edge), blocked (dotted, ends in a cross). No odds are drawn.

**Accessibility.** Palette in `src/ui/palette.ts`, applied as CSS variables; `tests/a11y.test.ts` computes WCAG AA contrast for every text and boundary pair (all pass). Skip link, one `main` landmark (the existing one in `index.html`, now named), `role=log` feed, `role=status` lines, text alternatives on every canvas, visible focus ring, tab order follows the page. Larger text and Reduce motion are stored settings; reduced motion also follows the system when the player has not chosen. Controls are at least 44 px. Signals that were colour only now carry a mark or pattern: gate line style plus ▣ and ◇, avoid hatch plus ⊘, danger ! and !!, edge of the map dashed with ?, feed lines with a leading glyph, status badges with ✓ and the like, low health as a hatched bar. A hand-written audit in `tests/browser/m9.spec.ts` checks names on controls, labels on inputs, heading order, one H1, unique ids and dangling references on the plan, run, Chronicle, history and history-entry screens.

**Onboarding** (`src/ui/onboarding.ts`, `src/ui/glossary.ts`). Six short guide screens as a card on the planner, never a modal. Skip is one press; the "seen" flag is stored; Help reopens it. A glossary drawer at the bottom of the planner defines the terms the feed uses.

**Audio** (`src/ui/audioCues.ts`). Cues for retreat confirmed, back at camp, shortcut opened, Gate Key spent, death, discovery, sanctuary (music steps down), level gained, and a boss phase function, `window.__EXPEDITION_AUDIO__.playBossPhase()`, for M8 to call. All use existing CC0 files. Nothing plays until Enable audio is pressed. Music and effects have separate stored volumes. Every cue also writes a line in a live-region banner, whether sound is on or not.

**Performance and layouts.** See below.

Other changes in `expeditionScreen.ts`: the play loop spends at most about 12 ms per frame stepping the run and caps its backlog, so a heavy fight at 16x slows playback a little instead of freezing the page. The step order is unchanged, so runs are identical. Help and Past expeditions are disabled while an expedition is out. `src/ui/expedition.css` was rewritten around variables and em sizes; it also overrides the global `#result` rule in `src/style.css` that was shrinking the Chronicle card to 11 px, and to 9 px on a landscape phone.

## Performance budget

Machine: this cloud container, Node 22.22, headless Chrome 156. Budgets are in `tools/perf-budget.ts` and enforced by `tests/perf.test.ts` and `tests/browser/perf.spec.ts`. `npx tsx tools/perf-report.ts` prints the numbers.

| What | Measured | Budget |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headless Old Road expedition (Causeway and Watchpost, 5 rooms), median of 9 | 230 ms (worst 430 ms) | 1000 ms |
| Per fight inside it, median | 57 ms (worst 108 ms) | 400 ms |
| JavaScript, gzip (one file, 1.66 MB raw) | 445 KB | 600 KB |
| 16x playback, longest main-thread task | 144 ms in the last run, 264 ms before the frame cap | 400 ms |
| Heap after 3 and after 20 expeditions in one page | 23.2 MB and 23.8 MB (+2.5%) | +50% |

Findings that are not green:

- At 16x the page still has a few long tasks (3 to 5 in a 15 to 22 s run) and one frame gap of 0.5 s. The cap helped (264 ms to 144 ms for the longest task) but one fight step is still heavy enough to hold a frame. Median frame time is 16.7 ms and the 95th percentile is 33 ms. This is a desktop-class number; a phone will be slower.
- The single bundle is 1.66 MB raw and Vite warns it is above its 1600 kB limit. There is no code splitting. The gzip size is fine for a prototype; the raw size matters for parse time on a phone and has not been measured there.
- Heap growth over 20 expeditions is flat in this test. It used the memory store; browser storage in a real session also keeps the history, capped at 1.5 MB.

## Layouts

`tests/browser/m9.spec.ts` checks the guide, planner with map, run view, Chronicle and history at 390x844, 844x390, 360x640, 768x1024 and 1280x720: no sideways scroll, no control clipped at the sides, every button, select, summary, input and download link at least 44 px high (and wide, except range sliders), no text under 11.5 px. It also checks 360x640 with larger text. Full-length software screenshots are in `docs/slice/screens/`. Re-take them with `M9_SHOTS=1 npx playwright test tests/browser/m9.spec.ts -g "layout at"`. They are Chrome captures at those sizes, not device photos.

## Known gaps and honest notes

- The feed still says "Banking comes with M3" on arrival at camp. That text is in `src/expedition/expedition.ts`, outside this milestone, and is stale.
- Cue-to-file choices are a first pass using the files already shipped. They have not been heard.
- The boss-phase cue exists but nothing calls it until the boss work wires it up.
- The Help guide is plain-language but nobody has tried it on a new player.
