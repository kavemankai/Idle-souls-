Combat bench deep-stats split

Split branch: an 18-stat line. Sword arm, shield arm, legs and body change what the adventurer’s body can do; five mind stats add back the AI’s mistakes. Change a stat and the fight restarts on the same seed, so you see exactly what that point bought. Against groups, the ring marks the one it’s focused on. Squares are archers. An amber shield is a Sentry’s guard stance: a light attack into it gets countered, a heavy breaks it. A green flash is the adventurer’s parry window; after a dodge that makes an attack miss it can follow up with a fast rolling slash.
